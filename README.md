# Your AI OOTD Wardrobe

Can't decide what to weaar for the day? No worries! Let AI do the job for you.

# 5 Steps to generate your OOTD
1. Open the [website](https://lizonair.github.io/Ai_OOTD/)

2. Simply drag and drop an image of your clothes into each categories.

3. Go to the OUTFITS tab.

4. Select the occasion that you like.

5. Generate outfits.

TADA Now you have a bunch of ideas for your OOTD!
